# DigiDexPlus Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 2 November 2026*

DigiDexPlus ("the app") is a free Digimon encyclopedia published on Google Play by DICEMZ. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

## The short version

- The app has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address, contacts, photos or files.
- We (DICEMZ) do not run our own servers for the app and do not collect or store personal data about you.
- The app shows ads from **Google AdMob**. Google's ads software in the app collects some device and usage data to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, as described below.

## Digimon data

To show the encyclopedia, the app downloads Digimon names, images and details from the public [Digi-API](https://digi-api.com/). Like any web server, Digi-API receives your device's IP address when the app connects to it. Searching happens on your device: what you type is not sent anywhere.

## Stored on your device

The Digimon you mark as favourites, and copies of the images the app has already shown, are kept only on your device so the app works faster and uses less data. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes them.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show banner and full-screen ads. The SDK collects and shares the following with Google:

- **Device or other identifiers**, such as your device's advertising ID
- **Approximate location**, derived from your IP address
- **App interactions**, such as which ads are shown and tapped
- **App info and performance data**, such as crash logs and diagnostics

Google uses this data to serve and measure ads, for analytics, and to prevent fraud and abuse. Learn more in [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown.

**Your choices:** you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

## Data security

All data the app sends, to Digi-API and to Google, is transferred over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
